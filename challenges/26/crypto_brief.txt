OPERATION CIPHERFALL — Cryptanalysis Brief
==========================================

A junior dev built a "one-time pad" that is really an LCG-keystream XOR cipher.

Keystream (Linear Congruential Generator):
  x_{n+1} = (a * x_n + c) mod m
  a = 1103515245
  c = 12345
  m = 2147483648
  Each step outputs one byte:  (x >> 16) & 0xFF
  That byte is XORed against the plaintext byte.

The seed is unknown but small: 0 <= seed <= 65535.

Ciphertext (hex):
  e958f315878a1a8e6030865cb0c1f3e869bb50e53b462dc1b33be7f97f536ea0c88735

Recover the plaintext. (Hint: the plaintext begins with the flag prefix.)
